    • 1948: Truman desegregates armed forces【25†L45-L53】.
    • 1954: Brown v. Board ends school segregation【27†L85-L94】.
    • 1955-56: Montgomery Bus Boycott (R. Parks → end bus segregation)【29†L225-L234】.
    • 1963: March on Washington, “I Have a Dream” (peaceful mass protest).
    • 1964: Civil Rights Act (LBJ signs) bans segregation【35†L46-L54】.
    • 1965: Voting Rights Act eliminates literacy tests【37†L75-L83】.

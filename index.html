Leaving Certificate / History

History essay plans

A collection of past Leaving Certificate questions, organised into clear essay plans. Open a question to explore its main arguments, supporting evidence and optional paragraph ideas.

Use the headings to choose your essay structure. The extra paragraph options give you more angles to discuss; you do not need to use every section. Expand the bullet points into your own sentences in the exam.

Study tip: learn the paragraph headings first, then attach two or three dates or examples to each one. You do not need to memorise every sentence.

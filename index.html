Chapter 9 — The Target Market

1. Explain the five stages of the product life cycle.
The five product life cycle stages are development, introduction, growth, maturity and decline.
2. Identify examples of products/services with different life cycle lengths.
Short life cycle: fidget spinners. Medium life cycle: a smartphone model. Long life cycle: a staple food brand or essential service.
3. Explain the terms market research and target market.
Market research is the gathering and analysing of information about customers, competitors and market trends. A target market is the specific group of customers a business aims its product or service at.

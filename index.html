Chapter 9 — The Target Market

Chapter 9
1. Identify three examples of products or services with life cycles of different lengths.
Examples include smartphones, fashion trends, and household appliances. Smartphones often have short life cycles due to rapid technological change, fashion trends may last only a season, while household appliances can remain in use for many years.
2. Explain what is meant by market research.
Market research is the process of collecting, recording and analysing information about customers, competitors and the market in order to help businesses make informed decisions.

Chapter 10 — Operations and Finance

Chapter 10
1. Describe five elements of the operational model of a manufacturer or dairy.
Inputs, processes, outputs, feedback and control systems are key operational elements. Inputs include raw materials and labour, processes transform resources, outputs are finished goods, feedback helps improve quality and control systems monitor performance.
2. Outline the meaning of supply chain management.
Supply chain management involves coordinating the flow of materials, information and products from suppliers to customers in the most efficient and cost-effective way possible.

Chapter 11 — Growth, Development and Expansion

Chapter 11
1. Explain what is meant by competition.
Competition occurs when businesses attempt to attract the same customers by offering similar products or services.
2. State the five forces that make up Porter’s Five Forces Model.
Competitive rivalry, threat of new entrants, threat of substitutes, bargaining power of buyers and bargaining power of suppliers.

Chapter 13 — Leading and Managing an Organisation

Chapter 13
1. Describe the role of a manager in an organisation.
A manager plans, organises, leads and controls business resources in order to achieve organisational goals efficiently and effectively.
2. Explain what is meant by delegation.
Delegation involves assigning responsibility and authority to employees to complete tasks while the manager remains accountable for the final outcome.

Chapter 14 — Leading and Managing People

Chapter 14
1. Explain what is meant by equal diversity and inclusion during recruitment.
Equal diversity and inclusion means ensuring all applicants are treated fairly and have equal opportunities regardless of gender, age, race, disability or background.
2. Distinguish between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation.
Intrinsic motivation comes from internal satisfaction such as achievement or enjoyment, while extrinsic motivation comes from external rewards such as pay or bonuses.

Chapter 18 — Being Informed and Making Informed Decisions

Chapter 18
1. Explain what is meant by fintech.
Fintech refers to technology-based financial services that improve or automate banking, investing, payments and other financial activities.
2. Explain what is meant by a neobank.
A neobank is a digital-only bank that operates online without physical branches and provides banking services through apps and websites.

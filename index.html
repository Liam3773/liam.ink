Essay plans

Choose a past question to open its individual essay plan. Each plan is split into an introduction, body paragraphs and conclusion, with key facts, names and dates to learn.

Use the bullet points as memory prompts. Expand each point into your own sentences in the exam.

Study tip: learn the paragraph headings first, then attach two or three dates or examples to each one. You do not need to memorise every sentence.

Flexible opinion-piece structure

  1. Introduction: introduce the issue and give your opinion.
  2. Causes: poverty, unemployment, discrimination and exclusion.
  3. Consequences: violence, crime, damage and fear.
  4. Solutions: education, opportunities and government investment.
  5. Conclusion: restate your opinion and call for change.

Adapt the paragraphs to the exact question. You do not need to include every theme.

Useful grammar

Three tenses to know

These will carry most of your holiday diary.

Passé composé

For completed actions and events.

J'ai visité Paris.

I visited Paris.

Imparfait

For descriptions, background and ongoing states.

Il faisait beau.

The weather was nice.

Futur simple

For what will happen in the future.

J'y retournerai.

I will return there.

Put all three together

Il faisait très beau et nous étions très heureux. Nous avons visité plusieurs monuments et nous avons pris beaucoup de photos. Je garderai de merveilleux souvenirs.

It was very sunny and we were very happy. We visited several monuments and took lots of photos. I will keep wonderful memories.

Opinion Piece Paragraph (French Riots)

À mon avis, il est indéniable que les jeunes se sentent exclus de la société. In my opinion, it is undeniable that young people feel excluded from society. La pauvreté et le chômage jouent un grand rôle et il existe un fossé entre les riches et les pauvres. Poverty and unemployment play a big role and there is a gap between the rich and the poor. Les réseaux sociaux peuvent influencer les jeunes et les gens ne se sentent plus en sécurité. Social media can influence young people and people no longer feel safe. La violence ne résout jamais rien, donc il faut prendre les mesures nécessaires. Violence never solves anything, so we must take the necessary measures. Il est essentiel de lutter contre les inégalités et nous devons construire une société plus juste. It is essential to fight against inequality and we must build a fairer society.
